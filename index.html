Our Story

Bantaba Kitchen started as a simple idea: to creat a place where people can enjoy good Gambian food, feel welcome, and spend quality time with people they love. Our passion for food inspired us to create a kitchen that brings the taste of home to every table. We want Bantaba kitchen to be more than just a place to eat. We want it to be a place where people can gather, share meals, and create good memories.

About Us

Bantaba Kitchen is a local restaurant located in Kanifing Estate that bring people together through delicious food and warm hospitality. We celebrate our culture by creating a welcoming place for family and friends to gather.

What We Cook

We serve traditional Gambian and West African food such as Super Kanja, Fufu, Domoda, Plasas, Jollof Rice and many more, prepared with fresh ingredients and authentic flavours.
